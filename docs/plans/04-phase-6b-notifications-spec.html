1A: Floating Drop Panel

Anchored directly below the bar’s notification pill (380px width, max 520px height, 9px radius). Feels lightweight, leaves wallpaper/windows visible.

1B: Full-Height Edge Drawer

Anchored from top screen edge to bottom (380px width, 0px margin). Provides maximum vertical room for deep notification history and scrolling.

1C: Slide-in Modal + Dim

Slide-in right panel with a subtle darkened backdrop over your desktop windows. Focused triage modal that closes on outside click.

Decision 2: Grouping & Organization Model

How should incoming notifications be ordered and clustered in the list?

Notifications 4
2A: Collapsible App Accordions

Clustered by sender app (e.g. Slack (2), Thunderbird (2)). Groups expand/collapse and have a per-group "Clear App" button.

2B: Flat Chronological Stream

Simple reverse-chronological list (newest on top). Individual cards with app tag pills. Zero nested folding complexity.

2C: Priority-Sorted Feed

Critical/Urgent alerts pinned at the top with glowing borders, followed by regular notifications grouped into "Recent" and "Earlier".

Decision 3: Inline Quick Reply Interaction

How should users type and submit quick replies to messaging notifications?

Direct Message 1
3A: Click-to-Reveal Input

Card shows a "Reply" pill. Clicking it slides open a sleek text field with auto-focus. Enter sends reply and dismisses card; Esc cancels.

3B: Pre-rendered Persistent Input

Cards from chat apps (Slack, Matrix, Discord) always show the reply field already expanded. Zero clicks needed to start typing.

3C: Quick Canned Action Chips

Presents one-click responses (e.g. "👍 On it", "Will check soon") alongside a custom reply field for fast triage.

Decision 4: Widget Integration Scope

Should the notification center include media playback controls or volume/brightness sliders?

Notifications
4A: Pure Notification Focus

Dedicated solely to notification triage and history. Audio/Media remains in the top bar. Lowest memory footprint and simplest code.

4B: Hybrid + Top Media Player

A compact MPRIS player card pinned above the notification list. Shows album art, track/artist, and play/pause/skip buttons.

4C: Full Control Center Hub

Consolidated hub with volume slider, brightness slider, media player, and notifications in one unified slide-out panel.
